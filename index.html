How to use this dashboard

Click a heading below to open it. The dashboard only reads the Concrete Log in Smartsheet, so nothing you do here changes the log.

1. What this dashboard shows
  • It compares what has been poured (from the Concrete Log) with the estimated volumes from precon's takeoff, by level and category.
  • Only POs with “Completed by Site Team” ticked count as poured. POs that are logged but not ticked yet show as “logged, not yet complete” and are never added to the poured totals.
  • The page does not update by itself. Click Sync now (top right) to load the latest Concrete Log. After a dashboard update, press Ctrl+F5 once to refresh the page properly.
2. The summary tiles
  • Volume poured (active) and Cost spent (active): poured so far, against the estimate for the levels and categories that have started.
  • Whole building poured and Cost spent (whole building): poured so far against the full takeoff and the full precon concrete budget.
  • Logged, not yet complete: volume and cost on POs that are not ticked complete yet. It is shown for information only.
  • Estimated costs by category use a blended actual $/m³ for each category, because precon has no budget per category. Treat the volume comparison as the reliable one.
3. By level and category (the cards)
  • Level opens a checklist. Tick one level, several levels, or use the quick choices at the top: All levels, All poured levels (every level with at least 1 m³ of concrete poured, counting completed pours only) or All completed (only the levels marked complete). The number in brackets shows how many levels each one picks. Then click Done (or click anywhere else). Clear unticks everything. Type and Mix are normal dropdowns. With nothing chosen, the page asks you to select a level or type.
  • Each card shows a ring with the % of the estimate poured, the cost spent, the poured volume of the estimate (large numbers), and what is remaining.
  • Colours: green on track (under 85%), amber near estimate (85 to 100%), red over estimate (above 100%).
  • Grey cards: “no estimate” (nothing budgeted for it), “level not mapped” (the level is not in the takeoff, for example L30 or above), or “none complete yet” (only pending POs so far).
  • A line starting “+ … logged, not yet marked complete” shows pending volume. A “Note:” line shows the first note written for that level and category.
4. The Level balance tile: over here, under there
  • It appears above the cards as soon as you tick a level. It adds up every category on the selected level(s) against the estimate, so being over on columns and under on slabs nets out to one over or under figure.
  • The headline shows the net, for example “So far: 6.3 m³ under”. Under it you see which categories are over and which are under, plus the volume and cost counted.
  • Level not marked complete: only categories that have started are counted, so an unpoured category does not show as “under”. Level marked complete: the headline says “Final” and every budgeted category counts, including ones never poured.
  • Logged is volume on POs that are in the Concrete Log but not ticked complete yet. Projected over / under (last column, and the smaller line under the headline) adds the logged volume to what has been poured, to give a rough idea of where the level will land if everything logged goes ahead as logged. It is an indication only: logged volumes can still change. Projected volumes also include back up pours, so the projected over / under will change as pours take place and back ups are removed (a note above the Notes area says this). Once a level is marked complete its figures are final, so the projected columns, the projected headline line, the projected total and that note disappear for it.
  • With several levels ticked, the tile combines them, with a By category table and a By level table.
5. Marking a level complete
  • Open the Level completion section. Each level has a bar showing poured vs estimate, with a Mark complete button. Use it when the level's concrete is finished. The button asks you to confirm: click Yes, mark complete (or Cancel). If some of that level's POs are still not ticked complete in the log, the confirmation warns you.
  • A completed level gets a green “✓ complete” tag, and its balance becomes the Final over or under. The top of the section adds up all completed levels, with volume and cost over or under the estimate.
  • Reopen needs a second click on Yes, reopen, so a level cannot be reopened by accident. Cancel keeps it complete.
  • Marks are saved on the site and shared. On another device they can take up to a minute to appear.
  • Tower progress (at the top of the page) shows the model photo. The tower and the front pavilion stay grey until their levels are marked complete, and each level you mark complete turns its floor to full colour, so you can see the building filling in. The surroundings are always in colour. Hover over the building for a level's status. Each coloured band is the storey whose floor is that level's slab; L25 is the roof deck and L26 and L27 are the penthouse blocks. The front pavilion goes with L1 (ground storey), L2 (upper storey) and L3 (roof). P2 and P1 are below ground and not in the photo, so they count in the total but are not drawn.
  • If a level is marked complete but some of its POs are still not ticked complete in the log, a warning tells you the final figures may still change.
6. Notes: explaining why something is over or under
  • Notes are written in the Level balance tile (tick a level first). Choose the level, then the category (or “Whole level”, or “Whole project”), add your name if you like, type the note, and click Add note.
  • Notes show in the tile for the ticked levels, and the first note for a level and category also shows on its card.
  • Delete needs a second click on Yes, delete. There is no edit: delete the note and add it again.
  • Notes are saved on the site and visible to anyone who opens the dashboard.
7. By precon estimate mix line
  • This list compares the mixes actually ordered with precon's mix lines. With nothing selected it covers the whole project. With a level, type or mix selected it shows the volume and cost for that selection, with the project-wide budget beside it for context.
  • Some precon lines are combined where one mix serves several lines, for example “#12 + #17”.
  • The LPM / line-pump mixes line is flagged for review on purpose: estimating did not expect the 45 MPa LPM, and it was used in lieu of puddle mix. The note under it says so.
8. How the estimates are built
  • Estimates come from precon's concrete takeoff, by level and category.
  • The slab estimate includes all the flatwork on a level: steps, toppings, stairs & landings, housekeeping pads and pedestals are added to the slab volume (Suspended slab, or Slabs on grade on P2).
  • Walls covers perimeter (shotcrete), interior below-grade, core & shear, walls above grade and stair walls.
  • P1a is counted as P1, because the log's Level list has no P1a. Log level codes such as L1 and L2 mean Level 1 and Level 2.
  • The P2 blinding line is kept as it is in the estimate.
9. Keeping the Concrete Log clean (this is what makes the numbers right)
  • Tick Completed by Site Team once a pour is done. Until then it is only counted as pending.
  • Use one location per mix slot. If a pour covers two locations (for example columns and walls), split the volume between Mix #1 and Mix #2 with one location each. A PO with two locations ticked lands in “Unmapped - review” and has no estimate.
  • Pick the right level. Use P1 (not P1a) for the first parkade level.
  • LPM mixes should not be logged against Columns. That is a data-entry mistake to correct in Smartsheet.
10. Other things you can do
  • Hide or show a section: click its heading bar (the arrow on the right). The dashboard remembers your choice on this device.
  • Pour Map: opens the pour map page, where pours are drawn on the level drawings. That section is still under construction and may be incomplete or inaccurate, so the button first shows a warning: click I understand, open the pour map to go there, or Cancel to stay. Its Dashboard button brings you back here.
  • Schedule of Values: the contract concrete mix rates and additional charges, for reference.
  • Amber message above Level completion or Notes: the site's saved data could not be reached, so your change is kept on this device only. It is sent automatically when the connection comes back. If it keeps appearing, tell whoever looks after the dashboard.
