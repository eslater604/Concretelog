One-time import: Level P2 pours

Loads the shapes from your Bluebeam markup onto the pour map. Delete this page from the site afterwards.

1. Unlock

Importing writes to the live pour map, so it needs the same edit password as the map.

2. Check what was found

Shapes are drawn on the built-in P2 drawing, numbered to match the list below.

#POFrom your markupFrom SmartsheetShapesStatus

Date, mix and cost always come from Smartsheet, matched by PO number. Rows that are ticked will be imported. Nothing is saved until you press Import.

3. Import

Finished. Open the pour map to check the result, then delete import-p2.html from your site so it can't be run again.
